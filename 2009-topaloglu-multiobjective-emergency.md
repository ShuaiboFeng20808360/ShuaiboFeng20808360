**Summary:** This research formulates a multi-objective MILP for scheduling emergency residents, considering service coverage, work-hour limits, and educational requirements. The model is solved via weighted sum and goal programming. Experiments on a Turkish hospital dataset demonstrate trade-offs between competing objectives.
