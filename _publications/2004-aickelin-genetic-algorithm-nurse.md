**Summary:** This research proposes an indirect genetic algorithm (GA) for nurse rostering. Instead of encoding schedules directly, the GA encodes rules or patterns, which are then decoded into feasible schedules. This approach reduces the search space and improves solution quality. The method is evaluated on real-world nurse scheduling instances, showing better performance than direct encoding GAs.
