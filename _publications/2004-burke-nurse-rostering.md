**Summary:** This is a foundational survey of nurse rostering. It categorizes problem variants (e.g., cyclic, rotating shifts) and solution approaches (exact, heuristic, metaheuristic). The paper highlights the gap between academic models and real-world practice, noting that most published systems are never deployed.
