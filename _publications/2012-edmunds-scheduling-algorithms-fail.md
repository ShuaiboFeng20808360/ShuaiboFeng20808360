**Summary:** This paper examines the reasons scheduling algorithms fail to be adopted in real workplaces. Through case studies, it finds that oversimplified models, lack of user involvement, and unaddressed human preferences are major barriers. It suggests that only 17% of research papers lead to deployed systems.
