**Summary:** This survey classifies personnel scheduling problems into single-skill vs multi-skill, fixed vs flexible shifts, static vs dynamic. It identifies that exact methods become intractable for >50 employees, and hybrid metaheuristics are the dominant approach. The paper calls for more research on multi-skilled flexible scheduling with real-world data.
