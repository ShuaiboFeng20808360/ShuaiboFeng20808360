**Summary:** The authors propose a reinforcement learning (Q-learning) based scheduler that adapts to real-time changes (e.g., demand spikes, worker absences). The method is tested on small-scale instances and shows improved responsiveness compared to static schedules. It is one of the few papers combining ML with dynamic scheduling.
