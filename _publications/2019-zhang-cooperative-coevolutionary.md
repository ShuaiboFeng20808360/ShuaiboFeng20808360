**Summary:** This paper proposes a cooperative coevolutionary algorithm (CCA) for multi-skilled workforce scheduling. The problem is decomposed into two interacting subpopulations: worker-task assignment and task sequencing. The CCA evolves both subpopulations cooperatively. Experiments on benchmark and real-world instances show that the CCA outperforms standard GAs and MILP solvers in scalability.
