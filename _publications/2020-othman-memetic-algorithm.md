**Summary:** This paper presents a memetic algorithm (MA) for the multi-skilled project scheduling problem (MSPSP). The MA uses a specialized chromosome encoding and a local search hill-climber to repair infeasible schedules. Experiments on benchmark instances show that the MA outperforms standard GAs and many exact methods in both solution quality and runtime.
