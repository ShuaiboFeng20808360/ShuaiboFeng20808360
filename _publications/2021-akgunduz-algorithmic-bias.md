**Summary:** This paper systematically reviews how algorithmic bias manifests in automated shift scheduling systems. It identifies bias sources such as historical data inequality, preference aggregation, and constraint encoding. The authors propose ethical guidelines and bias-mitigation techniques including fairness constraints and diverse stakeholder involvement.
