**Summary:** This is the core paper of this review. It presents a two-stage framework (MILP shift design + memetic algorithm assignment) with a decision tree trained on 2000 expert/MA-generated samples. The MLMA achieves 71–78% accuracy in identifying appealing patterns, reduces employee usage by 3–5%, and doubles schedule similarity (OC 0.48 vs 0.29) compared to MA, without significant cost loss (p=0.15). Experiments use 30 real retail instances.
